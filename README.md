# Superstore Business Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Superstore sales dataset to identify trends in sales, profitability, regional performance, product performance, and customer sales.

The goal of the project is to transform raw sales data into insights and recommendations that could help improve sales performance, profitability, and customer strategy.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end business analysis process, from data preparation and SQL analysis to exploratory analysis, visualization, dashboard development, and business recommendations.

The analysis was completed using Excel, MySQL, Python, Tableau, and Power BI.

## Business Problem

The business wants to better understand its sales and profitability performance across regions, product categories, sub-categories, and customers.

## Tools Used

- **Excel:** Data cleaning, calculations, and initial analysis
- **MySQL:** SQL queries for sales, profit, regional, product, and customer analysis
- **Python:** Data analysis and visualization using pandas and matplotlib
- **Tableau:** Sales and profitability dashboard
- **Power BI:** Dashboard and visualization

## Key Findings

- West was the strongest region by sales, generating $725K, while the South generated $392K.
- Technology was the most profitable product category
- Phones generated the highest sales among sub-categories
- Copiers generated the highest profit among the sub-categories at $55.6K.
- Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies generated negative total profit, highlighting areas that may require further investigation.
- The top five customers by sales provide opportunities to study purchasing patterns and identify strategies that could encourage repeat purchases.

## Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions could help improve business performance:

1. **Investigate Regional Performance:** Analyze the factors contributing to stronger sales in the West and identify opportunities to improve sales in the South.

2. **Focus on Profitable Products:** Continue supporting high profit categories and sub-categories, particularly Technology and Copiers, while monitoring their sales and profitability over time.

3. **Review Negative Profit Sub-Categories:** Investigate the pricing, discounting, and costs associated with Supplies, Bookcases, and Tables to identify opportunities to reduce losses.

4. **Study High-Value Customers:** Analyze purchasing patterns among the top selling customers to identify opportunities to increase customer sales and create retention strategies.

5. **Evaluate Lower-Performing Products:** Monitor sub-categories with lower sales such as Fasteners and evaluate whether changes to their product strategy could improve performance.
